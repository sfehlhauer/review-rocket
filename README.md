# Review Rocket

A rapid-response reputation service that helps local businesses turn missed reviews into a steady stream of high-quality customer feedback. It installs QR and SMS review prompts, monitors new reviews, drafts personalized responses, and delivers a weekly reputation scorecard so owners can visibly outperform nearby competitors.

**Category:** Local Marketing
**Target Audience:** Dentists, med spas, home-service companies, restaurants, auto shops, and other local businesses with weak or inconsistent online reviews
**Revenue Model:** One-time setup fee of $500-$1,500 plus a $299-$799 monthly retainer, with optional per-location pricing for multi-site businesses
**Viability Score:** 91/100
